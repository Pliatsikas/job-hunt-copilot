# T06 — Simplify: guide the person, one action per step

**Status:** planned · not started

## Asked (owner, 2026-09-16)

"Πολύ περίπλοκη." The owner showed the app to their partner, who said the same: too much to
work out, too many separate things to press. It should guide you — press one button and the
right things happen, not five buttons in five places.

Evidence already in the database: one real sign-up from 2026-09-15 never verified their
email (likely the partner; likely spam), and the owner themselves could not sign in on a
preview because their account is GitHub-only.

## Shape (to be designed before it is built)

- A first-run path: CV → preferences → first search → first analysis, as one guided
  sequence with one primary action per screen, not four independent pages.
- "Today" becomes the place that says what to do next, for a new account as much as an
  old one.
- One "analyse and prepare" action on an application that runs analysis, then offers
  the letter and the tailored CV from the result, rather than three separate cards.
- Fewer words on every screen. The current copy explains the mechanism; the person wants
  the outcome.

Design first — screenshots of the proposed flow for the owner to react to — then build.
